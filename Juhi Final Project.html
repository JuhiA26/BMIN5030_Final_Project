Introduction

When speaking to Dr. Angela Aherrera, Assistant Professor of Biostatistics and Epidemiology we spoke about environmental factors and the effects of heavy metal on the human body. Dr. Aherrera works with occupational cohorts and noted that heavy metal exposure can occur though the workplace and food/water consumption. From this information I chose to include these heavy metals in the model. When speaking to Dr. John Holmes, Professor, Biostatistics, Epidemiology, and Informatics about this project he suggested that one can use informatics to help determine what factors are important in the development of NAFLD by creating predictive models with several different variables.
